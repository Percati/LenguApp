One challenge a week to lock in your German or English.

⚠ LenguApp is NOT a course. It doesn't teach from scratch: it helps you consolidate what you already learned in a course at your level (A2 to C2).

HOW IT WORKS
Every week, one skill and one topic:
• Vocabulary and expressions with audio
• A short mission to produce real language
• Three 5-minute micro-tasks
• An optional weekend challenge
• A ready-made prompt for an AI to correct your work (you choose which)

Skills come back several times a year at a different depth: repetition is the point.

ALSO
• A library to search and filter all vocabulary and expressions
• Starred favourites you can sort and filter
• A printable PDF sheet to bring to your teacher or language exchange
• German and English, A2 to C2. Interface in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Works offline: the app doesn't even ask for the Internet permission
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• It only reads the date (and the system language, if you choose it). It only stores what you star
• Free and open source (GPL-3.0; content CC BY-SA 4.0)

For people already studying a language who want a calm weekly routine.
